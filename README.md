# AI, Checked

By Tea Cuturilo

A short explainer website about artificial intelligence: what it is, its benefits, its risks and possible futures. Every claim on the page is labelled as a fact, an interpretation or a prediction.

Live site: https://teacuturilo.github.io/ai-project/

Repository: https://github.com/teacuturilo/ai-project

## Files
- `index.html` – page content
- `styles.css` – layout, colours and typography
- `script.js` – buttons that highlight one type of claim
- `assets/` – diagram and favicon (SVG)
